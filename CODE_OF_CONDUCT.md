# Community conduct

Termlinks should be a technically rigorous and welcoming project. Be respectful, discuss evidence rather than personalities, and assume that people arrive with different experience, language fluency, and access needs.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private information, and unsafe pressure to disclose credentials or terminal contents are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove participation that puts people or systems at risk.

Disagreement is welcome. Explain trade-offs, reproduce claims, and make room for correction. AI-generated text or code has no special authority; contributors remain accountable for what they submit.

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile. Report security vulnerabilities through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Ratul1997/termlinks/security/advisories/new), not a public issue.
